Technical hurdles are always coming up in life, but simple solutions can also be expensive. harebrain aims to be the painless entry point for solving the tech problems that get in the way of your passions by offering free, community help.

Here are some things that harebrian can help with, but also keep it real:

Automation

Making things happen on their own - like scheduling invoices or making bookkeeping quicker. These things are logical steps that you do every day that stop you thinking about more important stuff, like serving your clients.

AI

Not the kind where you delegate everything to a machine, just simple micro add-ons to other automation, making the more subtle tasks easier to automate.

Email

There's a small technical jump to make from the free 'gmail' or 'outlook' email account to one that uses your own domain name. Having your own email opens a few more doors, and lends extra credibilty. It also allows you to grow later and use departmental addresses like accounts@.

CRM

A 'Customer Relationship Managment' system can be a piece of software or it can be a way of working with spreadsheets and files. You'll hear advice to use the 'systems', you can also start without them and find your way to the one that suits you best, if you ever need one at all.

GDPR

...is not as scary as it sounds. The basic idea is to be completely upfront about how you're going to use the personal information you might collect. Informed consent is the key, and registering your intent.

Security

There are 'triavial' bits and 'crucial' bits of security, and it goes with GDPR as a duty to protect personal information. You're probably not going to get hacked... you're not worth it. But, as the song goes, there are fifty ways to lose your data.

Media

Images, videos and audio are not all created equal. File sizes can be surprisingly big. It's good to know what's possible and get control over compression, quality and bandwidth.

Bookkeeping

For your accountant to their job you need to do a bit of bookkeeping. You also need to have access to your own figures in a way that helps you to make decisions. As you grow your enterprise this becomes exponentially more important, and delivering what HMRC requires of you gets easier when you've got a system in place.
